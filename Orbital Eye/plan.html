01 Complete System Flow

01

Ingestion & Frame Transformation

Raw tracking data from Ground Radar / CelesTrak arrives in Earth-Centered Earth-Fixed (ECEF). Rotational Matrices and Skyfield shift the reference into the Earth-Centered Inertial (ECI) frame — the true inertial reference for Newton's and Kepler's laws.

Rotational Matrices → Skyfield → ECI
02

Propagation & Uncertainty

SGP4 and SDP4 algorithms resolve environmental perturbations: drag, J2 gravitational harmonics, solar and lunar gravity. Primary ($x$) and Secondary ($y$) objects carry full 3D Covariance Matrix ($P$) error ellipsoids.

SGP4 / SDP4 · Drag · J2 · 3D Covariance $P$
03

Encounter Analysis

Time of Closest Approach (TCA) is computed efficiently via the vector dot product of relative velocity and position ($\Delta V \cdot \Delta R$). The 2D B-plane encounter plane is constructed via cross products ($\Delta V \times \Delta R$).

TCA: $\Delta V \cdot \Delta R$ · B-Plane: $\Delta V \times \Delta R$
04

Mitigation & Control Loop

The Pyomo optimization engine calculates minimal-fuel corrective thruster burns to physically shift Object $x$ out of the collision corridor — closing the autonomous loop.

Pyomo Optimization · Thruster Constraints

Layer 03 · Integrated Overview

03 Unified Operational Paradigm

OrbitalEye operates as a closed-loop space traffic management system that transforms raw sensor data into actionable collision avoidance maneuvers. The pipeline begins with frame transformation to establish a physically meaningful reference, proceeds through perturbation-aware propagation with quantified uncertainty, employs vector geometry for rapid encounter screening, and concludes with constrained optimization to generate precise mitigation strategies.

This end-to-end workflow enables autonomous, physics-based decision making for orbital safety — integrating ingestion, physics propagation, mathematical encounter screening, and optimization into a single unified framework.
